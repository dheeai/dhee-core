# Brief

Topic: The explosive rise of generative AI, 2020–2025.
Audience: a general business audience — curious, not technical.
Tone: energetic, optimistic, data-forward, credible.

Tell a tight 4–6 segment story with concrete numbers. Each segment is one
infographic panel (charts, diagrams, big stat callouts — NO people or faces)
plus one sentence or two of spoken narration.

Key points to cover (use round, illustrative figures — this is a demo):
- Adoption took off fast: ChatGPT reached 100 million users in about two
  months, the fastest consumer product ramp on record.
- The market is scaling steeply: the generative-AI market is projected to grow
  from roughly 40 billion dollars in 2022 to over 1.3 trillion dollars by 2032.
- Capability compounding: model context windows jumped from a few thousand
  tokens in 2020 to over one million tokens by 2025 — a thousand-fold leap.
- Where it lands: the biggest enterprise use today is content generation,
  followed by code, customer support, and data analysis.
- The payoff: teams using generative AI report 30–55% productivity gains on
  drafting, coding, and summarization tasks.
- Closing thought: the bottleneck is shifting from making content to directing
  it — the new skill is taste and orchestration.

Keep every on-panel label short and punchy so it renders cleanly.
