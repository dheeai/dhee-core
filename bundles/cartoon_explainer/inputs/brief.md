# Brief

Topic: The explosive rise of generative AI, 2020–2025 — told as a fun, punchy
**cartoon explainer** (think TED-Ed / Kurzgesagt energy).
Audience: a general, curious audience. Tone: playful, witty, optimistic, but the
numbers are real and land clearly.

Format: a 6-scene cartoon story. Each scene is ONE vivid illustrated moment that
*dramatizes* a single idea with characters and a visual metaphor — NOT a chart.
Render the key number/phrase as bold in-scene text (a sign, banner, or screen).

Recurring character (keep CONSISTENT across every scene for continuity):
**"Bit" — a small, friendly, glowing rounded robot/spark mascot** (a cute
chat-bubble-shaped helper with big expressive eyes), who guides us through the
story and reacts to each beat. Bit appears in every scene.

The 6 scenes (same data each time — keep the numbers exact):

1. **The Fastest Ramp** — Bit rides a rocket labeled "ChatGPT" blasting off,
   zooming past a snail and a tortoise wearing "TikTok" and "Instagram" jerseys.
   Big banner: "100 MILLION USERS IN 2 MONTHS".

2. **Market Goes Vertical** — Bit waters a tiny glowing money-sprout that erupts
   into a giant beanstalk of cash skyrocketing off the top of the frame. Signpost:
   "$40B -> $1.3 TRILLION".

3. **The 1,000x Context Leap** — Bit holds a tiny sticky-note that magically
   unfolds into an enormous towering library of books/scrolls. Caption:
   "1,000x MORE CONTEXT" with small tags "1K -> 1,000,000 TOKENS".

4. **Where It Lands** — Bit conducts a happy little team of worker-bot helpers at
   labeled stations: "WRITING", "CODE", "SUPPORT", "DATA" (in that order of size).
   Banner: "WHERE AI WORKS TODAY".

5. **The Productivity Payoff** — Bit zips along on a turbo skateboard leaving a
   speed-trail, juggling tasks effortlessly, a cheering crowd. Big number:
   "30-55% FASTER".

6. **The New Skill** — Bit hangs up the "maker" hard-hat and picks up a film
   director's chair + megaphone, pointing the way. Caption:
   "FROM MAKING -> DIRECTING" with a small tag "taste + orchestration".

Keep on-screen text SHORT and bold. One clear metaphor per scene. Bit stays
visually consistent throughout.
