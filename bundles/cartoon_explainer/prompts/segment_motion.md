## Rules

- Focus ONLY on the segment whose id is below. Describe the motion for THIS scene.
- Be SPECIFIC and PHYSICAL about what animates: name the main subject's action, the

  pumps a fist, confetti pops, quick punch-in".
- Mention: (1) primary subject action, (2) Bit's gesture/expression, (3) secondary
  motion (particles, background, props), (4) one camera move (push-in, pan, tilt,
  whip). Keep energy high but motion coherent — no morphing or melting.
- 2–4 sentences of plain prose. NO markdown, NO headings, NO quotes around the whole thing.
- Do not restate the on-screen text or the numbers; describe MOTION only.
