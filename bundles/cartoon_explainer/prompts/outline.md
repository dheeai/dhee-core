## Task

Distill the brief into a tight outline for a narrated infographic video of
**4–7 segments**. Each segment should communicate ONE clear idea that can be
shown as a single rich infographic panel and narrated in 1–3 sentences.

Write the outline as markdown:

- A one-line **title** for the whole piece.
- A short **through-line** (the single message the viewer should leave with).
- A numbered list of segments. For each: a short heading and 1–2 bullets on
  what it conveys (the data/idea) and what it should look like.

Keep it concrete and faithful to the brief. Do not invent statistics that
aren't supported by the brief.
